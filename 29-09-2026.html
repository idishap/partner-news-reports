PPC Land

IAB Austria guide puts AI-label duty on agencies, not clients

27 Sep 2026

IAB Austria and law firm act legal Austria published a five-page guide on when AI-produced advertising needs visible disclosure under the EU AI Act, whose transparency rules apply from 2 August 2026. It concludes the agency operating the AI tool is normally the responsible party.

Why it matters for Frontify: If agencies carry the labelling liability, partner agencies need provenance and AI-labelling metadata to travel with assets. That is the case for Frontify's AI Labeling and C2PA work on the H2 roadmap, and a concrete reason to raise it with DACH partners.
